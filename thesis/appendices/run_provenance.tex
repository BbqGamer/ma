Two reporting conventions follow from this provenance. First, screening studies
are marked as such and are interpreted mainly as design-space maps. Second,
figures or tables based on best test accuracy are treated as optimization
trajectory diagnostics. They show whether a method reached a better checkpoint in
an existing run; they do not constitute a strict validation-selected deployment
protocol.
